# 2. Visual Direction for the New Website

## Local + International Representation
Use approximately:
- **50% Pakistani/local faces and healthcare work environments**
- **50% international healthcare environments**

Pakistani/local imagery should include realistic:
- Doctors
- Clinicians
- Hospital environments
- Clinics
- Consultation rooms
- Healthcare teams
- Professional medical work settings

The remaining imagery should keep the website internationally relevant and should **not exclude U.S. and U.K. healthcare audiences**.

## International Market Positioning
The website should not look Pakistan-only and must **explicitly retain the U.S. and U.K. markets** in its positioning.

The reason is that healthcare buyers in these markets place strong emphasis on **data privacy, patient confidentiality, security controls and regulatory readiness**. NourDoc’s existing privacy/compliance messaging should therefore remain visible and relevant to these audiences.

### United States
Retain **HIPAA-aligned** positioning to communicate that the product is designed with the privacy and protection of patient health information in mind for U.S. healthcare environments.

### United Kingdom / International Markets
Retain **GDPR-ready** positioning to communicate attention to personal-data privacy, handling and protection for U.K. and other GDPR-relevant healthcare environments.

Use the existing website language such as:
- HIPAA Aligned
- GDPR Ready
- Patient Privacy By Design
- Role-Based Access
- Audit Logs
- Secure Cloud Infrastructure

The new website should therefore present NourDoc as **locally relevant but internationally market-ready**, with Pakistan/local healthcare representation alongside clear U.S./U.K. privacy and compliance positioning.

Do not describe NourDoc as “HIPAA certified” or “GDPR certified” unless formal certification/status has been independently confirmed.
